Smarter alerts from the CCTV you already have.

Border Watch reads your existing camera feed and raises an alert when someone crosses a line, lingers in a zone, leaves a bag behind or enters a restricted lane. No new cameras needed.

Four rules, one engine

The same detection feeds every rule, so a site only changes the lines and zones it draws.

Line crossing

A tracked person or vehicle crosses a virtual boundary.

Loitering

A person stays in a buffer zone longer than the allowed time.

Abandoned object

A bag is left alone after its owner walks away.

Restricted lane

A vehicle enters an area that is off limits to vehicles.
